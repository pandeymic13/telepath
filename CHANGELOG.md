# Changelog

After an update, a card on the home screen shows the first 3 entries of the new version's section here, and opens the rest. Put the 3 most interesting changes first. The build stops if the `versionName` in `app/build.gradle.kts` has no section here.

Write entries the way Plume Launcher does, in ASD-STE100 Simplified Technical English:

- Write one idea in each sentence. Use 20 words or fewer in each sentence.
- Use the active voice and the present tense.
- Use the names that telepath shows on the screen.
- Start each entry with "- ". telepath shows each entry as one item. It removes `**` marks.

Put changes that are not released under `## Unreleased`. Rename that heading to the version number when you release.

## 1.0

- Your photos fill the top of the screen. A different one shows each time you return Home.
- Make it yours: set favourites, weather, inputs, sports and theme colours in **telepath.properties**, then build.
- Continue Watching shows only what you are watching, with the full picture and its title.
- The theme follows the sun. The wallpaper takes the colours of dawn, sunset and night, and of the weather.
- Scroll down for the widgets: your inputs with your console first, shortcuts, the weather and a stock watchlist.
- The watchlist finds a share that you type without its exchange. RELIANCE becomes RELIANCE.NS.
- The **Up next** line beside the clock shows the next match or race that you follow.
- App tiles in the style of Apple TV, in day and night versions. New apps get a tile and a group automatically.
- The clock and **Up next** change colour to stay readable on each photo. Busy photos get a glass panel.
- The screensaver shows your photos instead of the Google TV ambient mode.

telepath 1.0 is built on Plume Launcher 1.4. For the changes in Plume itself, see [Plume's changelog](https://github.com/aradhyas8/Plume_Launcher/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md).
